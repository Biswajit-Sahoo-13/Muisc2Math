How it works

The song was reconstructed as a chain of resonant-filter equations that turn a clock t into audio. Each equation is a tiny expression; together they reproduce the melody, chords and glide amplitudes of the original track. The player above compiles the equations with the same engine as dollchan bytebeat composer, so playback and exported WAVs match the verified reconstruction (correlation 0.9999 against the reference render).
