Tonal â†’ essentially exact

Sustained, harmonic, slowly varying. Hits 40 dB on the fixtures because that is where the fixtures end. A pure 440 Hz tone reaches 102.2 dB and correlation 1.00000.

Percussion â†’ wrong basis

On the full track the median tracked partial lasts 0.104 s â€” about 10 frames. 289 of 291 candidates are too short to model and are correctly pruned. Sinusoids are not a drum kit; the literature uses an explicit transient model (Sinola's OLA/PSOLA hybrid, or S+N+T).

The decision behind this: fitting more sinusoids to drums would push the number up while making the result no more convincing to the ear. 12 â€” ARCHITECTURE

How the modules connect

Two independent toolchains share five foundation modules. The RES pipeline is the current one; the exporters consume nothing from the older track.

RES pipeline loop internals foundation (shared) exporters entry points

Why RES needs no sandbox

The RES renderer evaluates fitted numeric parameters directly â€” it never parses an equation string. The older track does parse user text, which is why it needs safe_eval: an AST whitelisting sandbox with 32 allowed functions, default-deny on every node type, and explicit rejection of attribute access and subscripting.

Same word, different systems

res_bank and formula_bank share only a name â€” no code, no data, no imports. res_bank is a runtime tournament with no template table; formula_bank is a static dictionary of waveform families with a Pareto/complexity decision rule.
